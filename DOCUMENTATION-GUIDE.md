## 🎯 Presentation Delivery Tips

### Beginner Presenter?
1. **Practice in front of mirror** (weird but effective)
2. **Time yourself** - aim for 25 minutes of content + 5 min Q&A
3. **Speak slowly** - you know the material, audience needs time to absorb
4. **Pause between concepts** - let ideas sink in
5. **Make eye contact** - not with screen, with audience

### Slide Navigation Tips
- Navigate with **arrow keys** (smoother than clicking)
- **Leave slides on screen** while explaining (don't move too fast)
- **Reference the slides** in your speech ("As shown here...")
- **Let diagrams speak** - don't explain every element

### Handling Questions
- **Listen fully** before answering
- **Refer to documentation** if you don't know ("Good question, let me show you in the documentation")
- **Stay confident** - you built this project
- **Admit unknowns** ("That's a great question I hadn't considered...")

### Pacing Yourself
- **Slides 1-3**: 5 min (move quickly through intros)
- **Slides 4-6**: 7 min (emphasize architecture)
- **Slides 7-12**: 8 min (deep dive on patterns - this is your expertise)
- **Slides 13-16**: 4 min (technical details, move briskly)
- **Slides 17-18**: 1 min (wrap up) + 5 min Q&A

## 🤔 Expected Questions & Quick Answers

### Technical Questions
**"Why PERN instead of MERN?"**
- "Relational structure matters here. Users have interconnected ratings, reviews, watchlists. PostgreSQL handles relationships natively."

**"How do you handle the 23 endpoints?"**
- "Good organization through middleware. Each endpoint has single responsibility. Middleware abstracts auth concerns."

**"What about scaling?"**
- "Multiple layers: Redis cache, read replicas, horizontal scaling, CDN assets, potentially microservices."

### Feature Questions
**"Why a 5-step recommendation wizard?"**
- "Guided experience feels more interactive than just typing keywords. Each parameter narrows down suggestions."

**"How does the profanity filter work?"**
